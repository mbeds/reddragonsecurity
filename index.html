Frequently Asked Questions

Cyber Security, AI Safety & OSINT Explained

Plain-English answers to common questions from business owners, founders, and managers.

What is Red Dragon Security and who runs it?

Red Dragon Security is a cyber security, AI safety, and online intelligence initiative by Matthew Beddoes in Telford, Shropshire, helping UK businesses spot and fix security weaknesses.

What does a cyber security consultant do for a business?

We check your websites, cloud accounts, email setup, and internal systems for weak spots, then give you a plain-English action plan to prevent hacks and data leaks.

What is an AI security and risk check?

We test how your business uses AI tools, chatbots, and automated workflows to ensure customers or outsiders cannot trick the AI into leaking private data.

What is OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) in simple terms?

OSINT means checking what information about your company, staff, domains, or servers is already visible on the public internet so you can lock it down before attackers use it.

How can we reduce our online digital footprint?

We track down forgotten subdomains, exposed staff contact details, and leaked public files, then help you remove or secure them to shrink your target profile.

Do you work with businesses across the whole UK?

Yes. While based in Telford, Shropshire, Red Dragon Security works remotely and on-site with businesses and founders across the United Kingdom.
