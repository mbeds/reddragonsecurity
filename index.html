Frequently Asked Questions

Plain-English answers on Matthew Beddoes’ background, practical threat intelligence, Azure lockouts, honeypot email traps, and AI risk protection.

Who is Matthew Beddoes and what is the story behind Black Dragon and Red Dragon Security?

Matthew Beddoes is a Telford, Shropshire cyber security consultant who gained early 2010s media attention as Black Dragon before dedicating his career entirely to defensive security—founding Red Dragon Security over a decade ago and appearing on BBC, Reuters, LBC, and Tripwire.

How does daily asset inventory and CVE checking protect a business?

Keeping a simple inventory of every website, server, plugin, and router you run—and matching it daily against newly published CVE vulnerabilities—lets you patch critical flaws within hours before automated scanners exploit them.

How do Azure IP restrictions and timed lockouts stop account takeovers?

Configuring Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) Conditional Access to allow logins only from trusted UK IP addresses, paired with smart timed lockouts after failed attempts, blocks overseas password-spraying attacks cold.

How does using an unused admin@ address as a trap catch phishing?

Creating an unadvertised decoy inbox such as admin@yourdomain.com that no legitimate supplier emails acts as an early-warning tripwire—any message hitting it immediately exposes active phishing or spoofing campaigns aimed at your staff.

What is practical Threat Intelligence for UK SMEs?

Threat intelligence tracks new software flaws, leaked employee passwords, lookalike domains, and active scams targeting your sector so you block threats proactively instead of reacting after a breach.

What does an AI Security & LLM Risk Audit check?

We test company chatbots, AI assistants, and automated workflows to ensure outsiders cannot use prompt injection to extract confidential files or trigger unauthorised actions.

How do you stop AI email spoofing and invoice fraud?

We enforce strict SPF, DKIM, and DMARC domain records alongside out-of-band payment verification rules so criminals cannot impersonate directors or alter supplier bank details.
